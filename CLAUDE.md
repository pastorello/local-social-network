## Project status

This repo is **Vivere Gaeta** (called *Gaeta Partecipa* through v1, renamed 2026-08-21), recentering (v2, 2026-08-21) from a civic-reporting app into a **curated cultural map-archive of Gaeta**: rich-text cards in typed variants (feste with processional routes, chiese, ricette, personaggi storici, dialetto), written by a small circle of admin-approved collaborators, readable by anyone, ultimately aimed at tourists. The authoritative spec is [docs/spec/README.md](docs/spec/README.md) — read it before making product decisions; scope changes start by editing it. [docs/mvp-spec.md](docs/mvp-spec.md) remains the record of the delivered v1 (map reports + polls — all still in the codebase). Decisions that would be expensive to reverse go in [docs/adr/](docs/adr/README.md); measured debt goes in [docs/tech-debt.md](docs/tech-debt.md); planned work goes in [docs/roadmap.md](docs/roadmap.md).

State: **the v1 feature set is delivered and tested** (spec §11/§13) — M0 through M4, ending with the Gaeta Partecipa rebrand (renamed **Vivere Gaeta** in v2), the Italian copy sweep, auth hardening and deploy prep. A security and quality audit on 2026-08-19 produced [docs/tech-debt.md](docs/tech-debt.md), which has grown to 43 items as later work found more; **all of them are closed** except the two the register itself marks open (TD-08, TD-37), plus all 10 documentation items — including every security finding and the whole CI story. **Read that register before proposing work.** **v2 phase C1 (accounts) is delivered** (2026-08-21): the "Collabora con noi" request/approve/invite flow and password reset replace v1's open signup — see the `account` bullet below. **Phase C2 (content core + feste) is delivered** (2026-08-22): sanitized rich-text cards, images, a procession-route map, draft/publish — see the `content`/`feste` bullets below and [ADR-0001](docs/adr/0001-jwt-in-localstorage.md)'s amendment. **Vivere Gaeta targets WCAG 2.1 Level AA, whole app** ([ADR-0005](docs/adr/0005-wcag-2.1-aa-accessibility-bar.md), 2026-08-22) — a target with a remediation plan, not a claim of present conformance; the ADR's Consequences section is the source of truth for what's fixed versus deliberately deferred. **Phase C3 (the remaining thin type apps) is delivered** (2026-08-22): `chiese`, `ricette`, `personaggi`, `dialetto` — see the bullets below and `content/api_helpers.py`. **Phase C4 (translations) is delivered** (2026-08-22): per-card translations (English first), the collaborator's "traduci questo articolo in…" control, and the reader's language switch — see `content/api_helpers.py::start_translation`/`visible_translations` and the `content`/`feste` bullets below. **Phase C5 (the map: layers & overlays) is delivered** (2026-08-27), completing the v2 build order: the new `maps` app, one toggleable layer per card type, and overlays in both kinds with the confidentiality gate [ADR-0006](docs/adr/0006-overlay-visibility-gate.md) records — see the `maps` bullet below. TD-02 (double Leaflet bundle) was paid at the head of that phase and is closed. **The design pass is complete** ([docs/roadmap.md](docs/roadmap.md) item 1, 2026-08-27 to 2026-08-31) and **TD-04's e2e coverage is now eight spec files and 47 tests** (2026-09-18), so the two sentences that used to stand here — "nobody has styled this product" and "e2e coverage is still one spec" — are history and are recorded as such rather than deleted: a reader who finds them quoted elsewhere should know they were true until the end of August. What "delivered" still does not mean is that the archive has content in it (roadmap item 4) or that anything is deployed (item 3). The design pass's spec is **[docs/spec/design.md](docs/spec/design.md) — read it before touching a stylesheet**: two audiences with opposite postures (logged-out reader mobile-first and primary; logged-in collaborator desktop-first and secondary), the mobile home as a full-bleed map with a bottom sheet, a register of "cultural magazine, not tourist brochure", the palette taken from Gaeta with the purple retired, and **colour in tokens, never in a component** — which is also what keeps the author's multi-city ambition from costing a second redesign. TD-04's e2e coverage deliberately followed that pass rather than pinning selectors on screens about to be redrawn, and landed after it. **The layout was reopened on 2026-09-11** (design.md D13–D21): the map under every page, pages as panels over it, the map reacting to what is opened and becoming the collaborator's editor. **All of D13–D21 is built (2026-09-18)** — see the "map shell" bullet under Frontend. **Deployment is last and happens on the author's signal**, not as a milestone that unlocks; media is served by the app itself through `common/media.py` ([ADR-0007](docs/adr/0007-serving-media-from-the-app.md), amending ADR-0003) — one hardened view, mounted in every environment so dev exercises what production runs, serving only the three image types `common/images.py` produces with the `Content-Type` from that allowlist, `nosniff` and a sandboxing CSP; a deployment still needs a persistent disk for `MEDIA_ROOT`.

Three v2 decisions with teeth, recorded in the spec: **no public signup** — the signup surface becomes a "Collabora con noi" request that the author approves person by person, so accounts are collaborator-only by construction (**built in C1**); **rich text is coming** — the SPA currently renders no HTML anywhere and ADR-0001 leans on that, so sanitization + an ADR revision are prerequisites to the first card render, not cleanup afterwards (**built in C2** — `content/sanitize.py` + the ADR-0001 amendment); **one content core, thin type apps** ([ADR-0004](docs/adr/0004-content-core-thin-type-apps.md)) — a new content type must cost days, not a module (**core + first type, `feste`, built in C2**). The old `posts` and `notifications` apps were **removed in M2** (their patterns live on in `reports`); the tutorial `Question`/`Choice` polls were **replaced in M3** by the spec §7 `Poll`/`PollOption`/`Vote` schema.

Standing decisions (recorded in the spec and audit): no chat/DM; no e-mail confirmation loop (an account is created active — first by v1 signup, now by admin approval in C1 — never by a "confirm your address" click); anonymous visitors get read access to map/reports/polls in M2, but the user directory and profile pages stay login-only; user e-mails are returned only to their owner via `/api/users/me/` and are never serialized elsewhere; UI copy is Italian (fully swept in M4), code/docs are English; the product brand is **Vivere Gaeta** (v2; it was Gaeta Partecipa in M4) while repo/package names stay `local-social-network`.

## Architecture

Two apps: `backend/` (Django REST API) and `frontend/` (Vue 3 SPA), talking over plain HTTP. `docker compose up --build` runs the whole stack (Postgres 17 + backend :8000 + frontend :5173). Bare-metal: backend falls back to SQLite, frontend defaults its API base URL to `http://127.0.0.1:8000` (`VITE_API_BASE_URL` to override); both servers must run for the app to work end to end.

Configuration is env-driven with dev-safe defaults — see `.env.example` (root, used by compose) and `frontend/.env.example`. `backend/backend/settings.py` reads `DJANGO_SECRET_KEY`, `DJANGO_DEBUG`, `DJANGO_ALLOWED_HOSTS`, CORS/CSRF origins, `WEBSITE_URL`, `FRONTEND_URL` (C1 — the SPA's own base URL, used to build invite/password-reset links; distinct from `WEBSITE_URL`, which is the API's), and switches to Postgres when `POSTGRES_HOST` is set. **`MAILERS`** (not `EMAIL_BACKEND` — migrated 2026-08-30 for TD-34, since Django 6.1 deprecates the whole `EMAIL_*` family and 7.0 removes it) follows the same `TESTING`/`DEBUG` gating as everything else: `locmem` under pytest, the console backend in dev, real SMTP otherwise. The environment variables keep their names (`EMAIL_HOST`/`EMAIL_HOST_USER`/`EMAIL_HOST_PASSWORD`/`EMAIL_USE_TLS`) and are read into the mailer's `OPTIONS`; **do not reintroduce any `EMAIL_*` Django setting** — with `MAILERS` defined, Django raises on one rather than ignoring it. **A mail server that does not answer is handled, not fatal** (TD-38/39, 2026-09-10): the admin's approve action is one `transaction.atomic()` with its send and rolls back with a message to the author, and password reset answers 200 for every address even when the send fails — a 500 there was an enumeration oracle. **Production has a `LOGGING` block** (TD-40): errors to stderr, `django.request` held at ERROR, because Django's default sends a production traceback only to `ADMINS` by e-mail, which here meant nowhere. Outside dev and tests, `CACHES` is the **database cache** so the credential rate limits (TD-18) are counted once rather than once per gunicorn worker; it needs `manage.py createcachetable` at release time.

### Backend (`backend/`)

Django 6 + DRF, thirteen apps:

- `account` — custom `User` (UUID pk, email login, no username; `role` = `citizen`|`admin`, default citizen, promoted via Django admin per spec F1.3; the internal `citizen` identifier is unchanged even though the v2 spec calls this tier "Collaborator" — a vocabulary shift, not a rename). `AUTH_USER_MODEL = 'account.User'`. **C1 (spec accounts.md):** there is no public signup — `POST /api/users/collaborate/` (public, honeypot + timing anti-spam, no CAPTCHA) files a `CollaborationRequest`; a `CollaborationRequestAdmin` action in Django admin approves or rejects it (approving creates the `User` with `set_unusable_password()` and e-mails a single-use invite link) or rejects it. `AccountToken` is one model shared by invite and reset (`purpose`, hashed token, `expires_at`, `used_at`; `AccountToken.create_for()` retires any prior unused token of the same purpose) — `POST /api/users/acceptinvite/` and `POST /api/users/requestpasswordreset/` + `/api/users/confirmpasswordreset/` consume it (reset requests always return 200, existing e-mail or not, to avoid enumeration). `User.is_steering` (spec politics.md) is a confidential capability flag, admin-settable only via `UserAdmin` — it must never be added to `UserSerializer`/`MeSerializer`. Its one consumer is C5's seggi overlay, read through `maps`' visibility gate ([ADR-0006](docs/adr/0006-overlay-visibility-gate.md)) — never through a serialized field.
- `content` — **C2 (spec content.md, ADR-0004):** the shared core every card type wraps, not an API of its own (no `urls.py`/`api.py` — a plain library app, like `common/` but with models). `Card` (UUID pk, `title`, `body_html` — always pre-sanitized, `author` FK `PROTECT`, `is_published`, plus `language`/`translation_of`, wired up in **C4**) has no type-discriminator field — a card's type is found via the reverse OneToOne (`hasattr(card, 'feste')`), not a stored field that could drift out of sync. `CardImage` (ordered, `common/images.py` unchanged) and `MapFeature` (`kind` = `point`|`route`, `position`, optional `label` — consecutive `route` rows form a polyline; `point` rows are independent pins) cover every type's map footprint in the spec's table without a schema change per type. `content/sanitize.py::sanitize_html` (`nh3`, strict allowlist, h2/h3 only — a card's own title is the page's h1) is the **one chokepoint** every type's write path calls; its plain-text counterpart is `content/fields.py::PlainTextField`, which the type serializers use for `ingredients`/`procedure`/`programme`/`meaning` and friends so a `<textarea>`'s browser-normalised CRLF does not become what the archive stores (TD-36); see the ADR-0001 amendment for why this is safe to render via `v-html`. **C3** added `content/api_helpers.py` (image save/remove, point-pin parse/replace, author-or-admin permission checks) and `content/serializers.py` (the shared `CardImageSerializer`) — extracted once a second type app needed the same write-path logic feste had inlined, so every type app (feste included, for images) now shares one implementation rather than one copy each. **C4 (spec translations.md):** `content/api_helpers.py::start_translation` is the single write path every type's new `<type>_translate` view calls — it forbids translating a translation (no chaining: the group is always original-plus-its-translations, never a tree) and creates the empty target-language `Card` + blank type row T2 describes ("traduci questo articolo in…" opens an empty version alongside the source), English (`en`) being the only entry in `TRANSLATABLE_LANGUAGES` so far (T1/T3 — more languages and machine-translation assistance are later work). **Three decisions of 2026-09-09 (spec translations.md T5/T6/T7) live in the same two files.** `content/serializers.py::media_source` returns the card that owns a group's photographs and map features — the original, whichever member you hold — and every type's Read serializer resolves `images`/`pins`/`route` through it, so a translation *shows* the original's and owns none; `content/api_helpers.py::reject_media_on_translation` is the write half, one guard behind `save_images`/`remove_images`/`replace_points` and feste's route, refusing loudly rather than dropping an upload. Every type's **public list** filters `card__translation_of__isnull=True` (one thing in the world, one row — the rule C5's map already followed) while **`mine/` deliberately does not**, since it is a collaborator's only way back to an unfinished translation. And every Read serializer carries **`original_title`** — the Italian name, which is the heading of a translated card too, with the translation's own `title` shown under it as a subtitle: the signs in Gaeta are in Italian, so a fully translated title is how a tourist fails to find the place. `CardArticle.vue` renders that pair and marks `lang` on the article and `lang="it"` on the heading (WCAG 3.1.2, inside ADR-0005's bar). `content/serializers.py::visible_translations` is the matching read-side helper — published-only for anyone but the card group's author/admin — that every type's Read serializer calls for its `translations` field (a `TranslationSerializer` list) alongside plain `language`/`translation_of` fields.
- `feste` — **C2, the first (and richest) thin type app**, proving the core per ADR-0004's "days not weeks" bar: `Feste` (`card` OneToOneField as `primary_key=True` — a Feste's id **is** its card's id, the classic Django one-to-one-as-subtype pattern), `recurrence` (free text, e.g. "Ogni 9 giugno" — not a computed calendar rule), `programme` (plain text, not rich). `FesteWriteSerializer` is a plain `Serializer`, not a `ModelSerializer`: it writes across `Card`+`Feste` at once, and images/route are handled in `feste/api.py` (`request.FILES.getlist('images')`, a JSON-encoded `route` string) rather than fought into DRF's multipart+nested-list handling. `GET /api/feste/mine/` exists because collaborators are not Django-admin `is_staff` (C1) — it's the only way they find their own drafts again. A draft 404s (not 403s) for anyone but its author/admin, so an outsider can't distinguish "doesn't exist" from "exists but hidden".
- `chiese`, `ricette`, `personaggi`, `dialetto` — **C3, the remaining four thin type apps**, each following feste's shape (models/serializers/api/urls/admin/migrations/tests) but calling `content/api_helpers.py` instead of re-implementing it: `Chiesa` (no extra fields; map footprint is one fixed pin, so its write path caps the shared point-parsing at `max_points=1` rather than a bespoke scalar field), `Ricetta` (`ingredients`/`servings`/`procedure`, all plain text; zero-or-more "sold here" pins), `Personaggio` (`life_dates` as free text — same rationale as `recurrence`, historical dates are often uncertain, e.g. "sec. XV" — plus a small-capped optional pins list for birthplace/monument), `dialetto`'s `Termine` (`meaning`/`example_usage` — **no `term`**, dropped 2026-09-09 because the card's title is the word and a reader saw it twice on every entry; **no map handling at all** — its `api.py` never imports the point helpers, matching the spec's "none" footprint for this type). Mounted at `/api/chiese/`, `/api/ricette/`, `/api/personaggi/`, `/api/dialetto/`.
- `libri` — **the sixth type (2026-09-09), and the first one nobody had planned for**: recommended books about Gaeta, proposed and specified the same morning (spec content.md), built the same day. `Libro` carries `book_author`/`year`/`publisher`, all free text — **`book_author`, never `author`**, because `Card.author` is the collaborator who wrote the card and a model holding both under near-identical names is how the two get confused. Its shape is copied from `dialetto` rather than from chiese: **no map handling at all** (a book is not a place; the library is, but that card would be about the library — and C5's rule means a sixth map layer would owe a sixth list too). Mounted at `/api/libri/`. **Covers are the publisher's or none, never a scan** (2026-09-09) — a rights rule no code can enforce, so it rides on the images field's own hint via `typeConfigs.ts`'s `imagesHint`, attached to the input rather than floating beside it. A second type proposed in the same conversation, `tradizioni`, was **dropped by the author within the hour** — every card he could name for it was already a festa or a ricetta; the reasoning is kept in the spec because it is the argument against the next generic type too.
- `luoghi` — **the seventh type (built 2026-09-18, spec content.md)**: the places a visitor goes that are not churches — beaches, the castle, monuments, museums, viewpoints, walks. `Luogo` is chiese's shape (one fixed pin, `max_points=1`) plus `genre` and `how_to_get_there` (plain text). **`Genre` is an admin-managed model**, like `reports.Category`, seeded by `luoghi/migrations/0002_seed_genres.py` from Google Maps' place taxonomy and addressed by **slug** everywhere (`/api/luoghi/?genere=spiaggia`, `/luoghi?genere=spiaggia`); `GET /api/luoghi/generi/` lists them with a published-count, and restates `order_by` because Django drops `Meta.ordering` from an aggregating query. **Churches are not a genre** — they stay their own type, and the reader meets them beside the genres (a *Chiese* chip on `/luoghi`). A translation shows its original's genre, as it shows its original's pin. On the frontend it is a `typeConfigs.ts` entry like the others, which added two generic capabilities: a `select` field kind whose options come from an endpoint (`optionsEndpoint`, rows of `{slug, name}`, shown through `displayKey`), and a `listFilter` that `TypeListView` reads from the URL query. Mounted at `/api/luoghi/`.
- `itinerari` — **the eighth type (backend built 2026-09-18, spec itinerari.md)**: curated tours. `Itinerario` (card + `form` `raccolta`|`percorso` + free-text `duration` + `intents` M2M) with ordered `Stop` rows (a **card that has a place** — never a translation — an optional `point_index` into its pins, a plain-text `note`); `Intent` is admin-managed and seeded (`mare`, `mangiare`, `citta`), listed with published-counts at `/api/itinerari/intenti/`. Stops/intents/path arrive as JSON strings beside the multipart images. **I4 lives in `api.py`**: publishing is refused while a stop's card is a draft, and the read serializer drops stops whose card was unpublished later for readers (the author/admin see them flagged). The walked path is the itinerary card's own `route` MapFeatures; a raccolta has none. A translation shows the original's stops, path, form and intents. **Not a map layer** — `maps.CARD_TYPES` is the list of types that *can be stops*, and itinerari is not in it. Mounted at `/api/itinerari/`. **Frontend built the same day:** `/benvenuto` (`WelcomeView`, the one address printed on B&B cards — never rename it), `/itinerari` (the generic `TypeListView` with `itinerarioConfig`, intents as filter chips), `/itinerari/:id` (`ItineraryDetailView`: the article with its stops, and the guided mode at `?tappa=N` — position in the URL, *Come arrivarci* from `lib/tours.ts::legDirections`), `/itinerari/nuovo` and edit-in-place through `components/content/ItineraryForm.vue`. Stops are picked on the map through `components/forms/StopsField.vue`, which lends `stores/map.ts` an editor of kind **`stops`** — the one kind whose clicks land on *cards' pins* (`editorPick`) rather than on the ground, and which does not dim the other cards. Itinerari sits in `AppHeader`'s pill between Schede and Il progetto; `seed_e2e` seeds "Itinerario E2E" for `itinerari.cy.ts`.
- `maps` — **C5 (spec map.md, ADR-0006):** the map's own app, mounted at `/api/map/`. Two public endpoints: `features/` returns every **published, non-translated** card that has a `MapFeature`, grouped one entry per card (`{id, type, title, points, route}`) so the SPA draws all five card layers (luoghi since 2026-09-18) from one unpaginated call — translations are excluded because a translated card is a second `Card` row and would put two pins on one doorstep. `overlays/` returns what *this reader* may see. `Overlay` is one model for both kinds the spec asks for (`kind` = `vector`, a GeoJSON document, or `raster`, a georeferenced image plus its `south`/`west`/`north`/`east` bounds), validated in `clean()` because the write path is Django admin — there is no collaborator-facing overlay editor, the author supplies the boundaries and the scans. **The gate:** `Overlay.objects.visible_to(user)` is the only place `visibility` (`public`|`collaborators`|`steering`) is read, it defaults to **`steering`** (the most restrictive tier — publishing an overlay is one explicit admin edit, never a side effect of creating one), and `OverlaySerializer` **must never serialize it** — a test asserts the word never appears in a response body. This is `User.is_steering`'s first consumer; read ADR-0006 before touching any of it.
- `reports` — the core domain: `Category` (admin-managed, seeded with 7 Gaeta defaults in a data migration), `IssueReport` (status workflow `open→acknowledged→resolved`, `rejected`; transitions in `ALLOWED_TRANSITIONS`, admin-only via `role`), `Upvote` (unique user+report, denormalized `upvotes_count`). Photo uploads go through `common/images.py` — a plain package, not an app — shared with `account` (and now `content`) so every upload gets the same treatment (TD-12): ≤5MB, JPEG/PNG/WEBP **by decoded content**, a 40 Mpx header check before `load()`, EXIF stripped after the orientation is applied, and the stored filename's extension rebuilt from the detected format (TD-13 — Django's own `validate_image_file_extension` allows 70 extensions, many of which are not `image/*`). List/detail/map/categories are **public** (`@permission_classes([])`) — JWT still identifies the user for `upvoted_by_me`.
- `polls` — spec §7 rebuild (M3): `Poll` (question ≤200, `closes_at?`, manual `is_closed`; *effectively* closed when either applies — see `is_currently_closed`), `PollOption` (ordered by `position`), `Vote` (unique user+poll, final — no changes). List/detail are **public**; create/close are admin-only; results (`votes_count`/`total_votes`) serialize as `null` until visible per F3.3 (voted / closed / requester is admin). List orders open polls first (F3.4) via a `closed_rank` annotation. Gotcha: the options prefetch must restate `.order_by('position')` because `annotate()` drops `Meta.ordering`.

Conventions: `models.py` / `serializers.py` / `api.py` (DRF `@api_view` function-based views) / `urls.py` per app; routing mounted under `/api/users/`, one prefix per card type (`/api/feste/`, `/api/chiese/`, … `/api/itinerari/`), `/api/map/`, `/api/polls/` and `/api/` (reports: `/api/categories/`, `/api/reports/...`) in `backend/backend/urls.py` — `content` has no mount point of its own (see above). UUID pks everywhere. Errors follow `{detail, fields}` via the global handler in `backend/backend/exceptions.py` — raise DRF exceptions, don't hand-roll error JSON. That handler also translates Django's own `ValidationError` into a 400 (TD-14 — the ORM raises it for a malformed UUID and DRF's handler doesn't know it) and rewrites throttle 429s into Italian. Credential endpoints are rate limited via `account/throttling.py` (TD-18); `backend/conftest.py` clears the cache between tests so the limits don't bleed across the suite. Every list endpoint paginates through `common/pagination.py::StandardPagination` (20/page, `page_size` capped at 100); `GET /api/reports/map/` is deliberately unpaginated and returns slim pins for the map. `django-debug-toolbar` is a dev dependency and is installed only when `DEBUG and not TESTING` (TD-24).

Auth is JWT (`rest_framework_simplejwt`): global `IsAuthenticated` default, so new endpoints are locked down unless you add `@authentication_classes([])`/`@permission_classes([])` (see `account.api.collaborate`). Hardened in M4: access tokens 60 minutes, refresh 30 days with rotation + blacklist (`token_blacklist` app installed — its migrations must run); the SPA refreshes transparently via the 401 interceptor in `frontend/src/lib/auth.ts` (one retry per request). **Single-flight lives in `stores/user.ts::refreshToken`, not in the interceptor** (2026-08-30): the interceptor is not the only caller — `initStore` refreshes on app start — so a guard inside it could not see that one, and a page load fired two refreshes with the same token. With rotation-and-blacklist on, the second could present a token the first had just invalidated, whose failure path clears the session. `cypress/e2e/session.cy.ts` and two store tests pin it. **A page load refreshes only when the access token is about to expire, and only a 401 ends a session** (2026-09-11, TD-42): `initStore` reads the token's `exp` through `lib/jwt.ts::expiresSoon` — a scheduling decision, not a trust one — because every refresh rotates, and a refresh whose page is torn down is still carried out server-side, so the next load presents a blacklisted token. `performRefresh` keeps the session on a network error or a 5xx, which say nothing about the token; that is why `account/serializers.py::TokenRefreshSerializer` (TD-43) turns a deleted account's refresh into a 401 instead of simplejwt's 500. The accepted cost: after a password change elsewhere, a reloaded tab looks signed in for up to the access token's 60 minutes. Reading CI's `api.log`: on Linux a request whose page died leaves **no line at all**, neither access line nor "Broken pipe" — absence there is not proof. **Logout ends the local session first, then tells the server** (2026-09-11, TD-41): `stores/user.ts::logout` calls `removeToken()` before posting the refresh token, and `/api/users/logout/` takes **no bearer token** (`@authentication_classes([])`) because the request now goes out after the access token is gone — possessing the refresh token is the proof, and blacklisting it ends only that session. The old order waited for the server, so a reload in between kept the session alive and its still-valid access token read a draft after "Esci"; that was `collaborator.cy.ts`'s intermittent failure on main from 2026-09-05. Blacklisting a refresh token never revokes an access token — that is why the local half has to go first. Production serving: whitenoise + `STATIC_ROOT`/`collectstatic`, gunicorn pinned, HTTPS hardening block active when `DJANGO_DEBUG=false` (see docs/deploy.md). Never put `email` into `UserSerializer` — it's intentionally omitted (spec F1.4). `/api/users/me/` is the only place a user sees their own email/role, and it goes through `MeSerializer(UserSerializer)`, which adds exactly those two fields; it used to hand-build a dict and spell the avatar `avatar` instead of `avatarURL` (TD-27). `User.get_avatar()` returns **None** when there is no upload — the SPA renders initials via `components/cards/UserAvatar.vue` (TD-30).

### Frontend (`frontend/`)

Vue 3 + Composition API, Vite, Pinia, Vue Router, Tailwind v4 (+ `@tailwindcss/typography` since C2, for `.prose` on rendered rich text), zod (with Italian locale) for form validation, Leaflet via `@vue-leaflet/vue-leaflet` + `leaflet.markercluster` (`components/maps/GaetaMap.vue`, centered on Gaeta; **leaflet is default-imported on purpose** — a namespace import loses the runtime-registered `markerClusterGroup` in production builds; `vite.config.ts` also aliases `leaflet/dist/leaflet-src.esm` onto the same entry, which is TD-02's fix and keeps vue-leaflet's fallback import from resolving to a second, unaugmented copy), Tiptap (`@tiptap/vue-3` + `@tiptap/starter-kit`, since C2) for rich-text authoring.

- **C2 content components** (`components/content/`): `RichTextEditor.vue` wraps Tiptap (`StarterKit.configure({ heading: { levels: [2, 3] }, link: {...} })` — StarterKit already bundles Link in Tiptap v3, a separate `@tiptap/extension-link` would just duplicate it); `RichText.vue` is the **only `v-html` in the codebase** (ADR-0001's amendment) — it must only ever render `card.body_html` as returned by the API, never anything else, since its safety argument depends on there being exactly one sanitizing chokepoint (`backend/content/sanitize.py`) feeding exactly one render path. The in-form map editors C2/C3 built — `FesteRouteEditor.vue` and `MapPinsEditor.vue`, on a `composables/useLeafletMap.ts` — **were retired on 2026-09-18** (design.md D19): a card's place is now drawn on the shell's one map, through `components/forms/MapPointsField.vue` (see the map shell bullet). No unit test mounts a real Leaflet map anywhere in this codebase (jsdom can't resolve Leaflet's marker-icon PNG imports): tests stub `GaetaMap`/`MapStage`, or test the store the map draws.

- **C3 generic type components**: `chiese`/`ricette`/`personaggi`/`dialetto`/`libri`/`luoghi` share one config-driven `TypeForm.vue`/`TypeListView.vue`/`TypeDetailView.vue`/`TypeCreateView.vue` set (in `components/content/` and `views/`) instead of four literal copies of the `Feste*.vue` files — each route passes its `definitions/typeConfigs.ts` entry (`endpoint`, `routeNames`, `extraFields`, `mapMode`) as a `config` prop. `feste` keeps its own dedicated views; its route and `recurrence`/`programme` fields don't generalize cleanly and weren't worth disturbing. A type's pins are edited with `MapPointsField.vue` (`kind="pins"`, capped by `maxPoints`) and a feste's route with the same field (`kind="route"`) since D19. `AppHeader.vue`'s nav replaced the flat "Feste" `MenuButton` with `SchedeMenu.vue`, a keyboard-operable disclosure menu (`aria-haspopup`/`aria-expanded`, closes on Escape/outside-click/route change) listing all card types (five at the time, six since `libri` in 2026-09-09, seven since `luoghi` on 2026-09-18 — itinerari is not among them, it has its own place in the pill) — that many flat nav items plus Utenti would have overflowed the nav row. **A new type is added in two lists, not one**: `SchedeMenu.vue` and `views/ProjectView.vue` (Il progetto, which replaced the footer on 2026-09-18) both enumerate them by hand, and `ProjectView.spec.ts` asserts every type's path is there, so a forgotten one fails there rather than in review.

- **C4 translations** (`components/content/`): `LanguageSwitch.vue` (reader-facing) and `TranslateControl.vue` (the "traduci questo articolo in…" collaborator control, T2) are shared across `TypeDetailView.vue` and `FesteDetailView.vue` — both read the same `translations`/`language`/`translation_of` fields every type's Read serializer now carries, so no per-type copy was needed. `TranslateControl` posts to `<type>/<id>/traduci/`, then `router.push`es straight to the new (empty) translation's own detail route with `?edit=1`; the target-language set is `lib/languages.ts::TRANSLATABLE_LANGUAGES` (English only today, per T1/T3). Both detail views gained a `watch(() => route.params.id, …)` for this: navigating card-detail-to-card-detail keeps the same route record, so Vue Router reuses the component instance instead of remounting it, and `editing` has to drop to `false` before the refetch resolves — `TypeForm.vue`/`FesteForm.vue` only read their `item`/`feste` prop once at setup, so flipping straight to edit mode would otherwise mount the form with the still-stale previous card.

- **C5 the map** (spec map.md): `components/maps/GaetaMap.vue` is the one map — reports (clustered, status colors), the five card layers, and both overlay kinds. It only draws what it is handed: `stores/map.ts` holds which layers are on (since 2026-09-18 — `HomeView`'s toggles write it, `MapStage` passes the filtered `pins`/`features`/`overlays` and the page's `focus`), so the toggles are plain computed filters and not map state. Overlays are added and removed **by id**, never rebuilt as a group, because the opacity slider fires continuously while dragged and a rebuild per tick makes a raster flash; they also get their own Leaflet panes (`z-index` 350/380, between the tiles and everything clickable) so a polygon can't swallow a pin's click. `lib/mapLayers.ts` holds the layer list (key, label, color, route) — `key` is deliberately both the API's `type` and the card's route segment, so a pin knows where it links without a per-type lookup. **The two text lists are the keyboard path a clickable pin does not have, and since 2026-09-05 they are the *decided* conforming path rather than a mitigation while the widget waits** (ADR-0005's map-widget deferral is closed, not outstanding): `PlacesList.vue` for the five card layers, `ReportsList.vue` for the reports layer — which had no list at all until then, so a report was reachable only by clicking its pin. Both render in both homes, both are filtered by the same `visiblePins`/`visibleFeatures` the map draws, and both carry an `aria-label` ("Luoghi sulla mappa", "Segnalazioni sulla mappa") that the specs select on. **Leaflet markers stay unfocusable on purpose** — `leaflet.markercluster` replaces markers with cluster icons as the reader zooms, so "every marker is in the tab order" is not a state this widget has. That is enforced, not hoped for, since 2026-09-08: `lib/leafletKeyboard.ts` sets `L.Marker.prototype.options.keyboard = false` once (cluster icons are built inside the library on every zoom, so there is no instance to pass an option to), and `a11y.cy.ts` asserts no `.leaflet-marker-icon[tabindex="0"]` survives. Before that the claim was simply false — six unnamed stops announcing as "2", "3", "6". The **container** stays focusable, because that is what arrow-key panning needs, and carries an `aria-label` instead of announcing its own text. If you add a layer to the map, it needs a row in a list too; that is now a rule, not a nicety. `definitions/interfaces/MapData.ts` mirrors the two `maps` endpoints and, like the API, **has no `visibility` field** — do not add one.
- **The map shell** (spec design.md D13/D15/D16/D17, **built 2026-09-18**): **one Leaflet map for the whole app**, `components/maps/MapStage.vue`, mounted by `App.vue` under every route and drawing `stores/map.ts` (features, report pins, overlays, layer toggles, report placing, and the open page's `focus`). **Every route but the home renders in `components/layout/RoutePanel.vue`** over it, shaped by `meta.panel` (`'narrow'` for card types with a place and reports, a wide centred panel over a veiled map otherwise; full screen on a phone). **`HomeView.vue` is chrome, not a page**: the home route renders `views/EmptyRoute.vue`, and `App.vue` keeps `HomeView` mounted (`v-show`) for the life of the app so focus can return to the link that opened a panel — it sits *after* the panel in the DOM on purpose (see design.md). The map is `inert` whenever a panel covers it. **A page makes the map react with `composables/useMapFocus.ts`** — never by touching the map — **and a form draws on it with `components/forms/MapPointsField.vue`** (D19): while the form's map tab is open the field lends its points to `stores/map.ts`'s `editor`, the map draws them, dims the rest, shows a "Stai disegnando…" banner with *Fatto*, and hands clicks back to the field; the form owns the data, so cancelling cancels the drawing. The typed-coordinates fallback is in the same field. `TypeForm`/`FesteForm` show their sections as `components/forms/FormTabs.vue` tabs (ARIA tabs pattern; a tab holding a rejected field says so, and a failed save opens it) — and **no detail view mounts a map of its own** — on a wide screen the map is beside the narrow panel; on a phone (D18) a narrow panel starts 180px down, the shell's map shows through as a strip above it, and `?mappa=1` is the enlarged state (so Back undoes it). `router/meta.d.ts` types the meta fields, but RouterView's slot does not see the augmentation, so `App.vue` compares `meta.panel` instead of reading it through.
- **The design pass** (spec [design.md](docs/spec/design.md), roadmap item 1, **complete 2026-08-31**): **colour and type live in `assets/main.css` as `@theme static` tokens and nowhere else** (D8) — no component writes a hex or names a font family, and `lib/tokens.ts::readToken` reads them back for the handful of callers that must hand a real colour string to Leaflet, each with the same value as a fallback so a token that fails to cascade degrades visibly rather than to `undefined`. `static` is deliberate: Tailwind otherwise emits only the properties its generated utilities reference, so a token the layout has not reached yet would vanish from the document. **Fonts are self-hosted** (D6b) — a Google Fonts `<link>` would reintroduce exactly what TD-30 and accounts.md A1 removed, and the app ships one `@font-face` with the latin subset rather than `@fontsource`'s three. **`HomeView.vue` is two layouts** (D2/D3/D4), switched in JS at 1024px via `composables/useBreakpoint.ts` rather than with `lg:hidden` on both — only one is ever in the DOM, because two copies mean two `id="layer-chiese"` (and, before the map moved to the shell, two Leaflet maps); `LayerToggles.vue`/`PlacesList.vue` are shared by both, `MapSheet.vue`/`MapChrome.vue` are the small-first pair. `AppHeader.vue` measures itself and publishes `--header-h`, which the shell's padding and the map's height both read — a hard-coded clearance put the map under the header. **The images row is `components/forms/CardImagesField.vue`**, shared by `TypeForm` and `FesteForm` (2026-09-07) — one ordered strip of thumbnails whether an image is already stored or was picked a moment ago, because D5 makes the **first image the cover** and that is an editorial decision, not the order a file picker happened to sort. The same two buttons order a feste's **route stops** (`MapPointsField`), where the order is not editorial but literal — consecutive stops are what the polyline joins — and both call `lib/reorder.ts::moveItem`, which is the only implementation. It is arranged with two buttons per image and no drag-and-drop: dragging cannot be the only way to reorder under ADR-0005, and the labels say where the image goes ("Sposta l'immagine 2 al primo posto: diventa la copertina"). The wire format is `lib/cardImages.ts::appendImages` and `content/api_helpers.py::save_images(card, files, raw_order)`: **`image_order` is a list of tokens** — an existing image's id, or `new:<n>` for the n-th file in *this* request — so a photograph being uploaded now can become the cover in the same save rather than after a round trip. A token nobody recognises is ignored and an image the order does not mention keeps its relative place at the end: a partial order arranges, it does not delete. Note `CardImage`'s pk is an **integer**, unlike the UUIDs elsewhere, which is why both ends stringify the token. **Forms wire their errors through `components/forms/FormField.vue`** (D2, 2026-08-30): it owns the label/control/error relationship so `aria-describedby`/`aria-invalid` cannot be half-wired, and `lib/apiErrors.ts::extractFieldErrors` keeps the `{detail, fields}` body *per field* instead of flattening it — that flattening is why every form used to show one undifferentiated list at the bottom. `TypeForm`/`FesteForm` use it, and since 2026-08-31 so do the seven auth and profile forms (`LoginView`, `CollaboraView`, `InviteAcceptView`, both password-reset views, `EditProfileView`, `EditPasswordView`) — an accessibility change, not a design one: they keep their v1 layout. Two rules came with that: **validation is attributed, never prefixed** — `forms/user.ts::parseZodObject` returns `fieldErrors` keyed by the field that failed, and the old "E-mail: …" prefix is gone because the message now sits under that label; and **what has no field stays form-level**, in `components/forms/FormErrors.vue` with `role="alert"` — login's answer is deliberately vague ("e-mail o password non corretti"), since naming which half was wrong would tell a stranger whether an address has an account here. **`components/content/CardArticle.vue` is the one reading surface** and **`CardGrid.vue` the one list surface**: `TypeDetailView`/`FesteDetailView` wear the first, `TypeListView`/`FesteListView` the second, because a feast and a church are the same *article* (and the same list row) to a reader — restyling that twice is how the two drift apart. `CardGrid` takes a normalised `{id, title, summary, cover, isPublished, to}` and knows nothing about card types; a card with no image gets a **tint** of its layer colour via `color-mix`, not the saturated colour, which D7 reserves for the map. **The credits surface D11 asks for is `views/ProjectView.vue`, *Il progetto*, at `/il-progetto`** (design.md D21, 2026-09-18) — it was `components/AppFooter.vue` until the map went under every page and no route was left for a footer to sit on. It carries the editorial rule, the card types, the polls and *Collabora con noi*. **The menu is `components/AppHeader.vue`, a pill at the top centre floating over the map** (D14): wordmark, Mappa, Schede, Il progetto, then Accedi or an avatar whose `NavDisclosure` holds the profile, Utenti and Esci; on a phone Schede stays in the pill and the rest sits behind *Menu*. It publishes `--header-h` as the distance from the top to just below the pill — panels and the home's chrome start there; the map does not, it runs under the pill, and Leaflet's own controls are pushed below it. **`ReportDetailView.vue` wears the article's skeleton without `CardArticle`** (a report has no rich text, no translations and no gallery, but it does have a status and a support count) — it was redrawn because a pin on the redesigned home pushes straight to it. What the pass deliberately left in v1 clothes — `/polls`, `/users`, `/profile`, the auth forms and `ReportForm` — is listed with its reasons at the foot of design.md; none of them is on the reader's path, and `ReportForm` is shared with the home's filing flow.
- **The shell carries a skip link** (`App.vue`, WCAG 2.4.1): the header is seven tab stops before any page content on every route, so the first focusable element in the document is a link to `<main id="contenuto" tabindex="-1">` — `sr-only` until focused. The `tabindex="-1"` is load-bearing: without it the browser scrolls to the target and leaves focus behind. **`ActionButton` takes a `to`** and renders a `RouterLink` with the same look rather than being wrapped in one — a `<button>` inside an `<a>` is two tab stops for one control and two announcements of it.
- Everything under `src/` is TypeScript (`strict` via `@vue/tsconfig`, `noUncheckedIndexedAccess` set locally in `tsconfig.app.json`); `npm run build` type-checks and must stay green. The Pinia stores (`stores/user.ts`, `stores/toast.ts`) are typed — `user` persists auth state (tokens, profile, `role`) to `localStorage` by hand, refreshes the access token on app init, and `refreshToken()` persists the rotated refresh token (M4). `lib/auth.ts` installs the 401-refresh axios interceptor (wired in `main.ts` right after pinia).
- `router/index.ts` gates routes via `meta.requiresAuth` + a global guard; `/` (the map — C5's landing view, h1 "La mappa di Gaeta"), `/reports/:id` and `/polls` are public, `/users` and `/profile/*` stay auth-only (M1 decision). Write actions check `userStore.user.isAuthenticated` in-component and redirect to login with an Italian toast; `LoginView` honors the guard's `?redirect` query after a successful login (M4). **C1:** `/signup` is gone — `/collabora` (`CollaboraView.vue`) is the public request form, `/invito/:token` / `/reimposta-password/:token` read the token from `route.params` and complete the invite/reset — and, on success only, call `userStore.logout()`, because whatever session this browser held must not outlive the handover (2026-09-11, spec accounts.md: the author trying an invite on their own machine otherwise left the new collaborator writing as the author), `/password-dimenticata` requests one. All four are public routes; there is no admin-review UI in the SPA (Django admin is the reviewer, per spec A2). **C2:** `/feste` (list, public) and `/feste/:id` (detail, public — the route guard doesn't gate drafts, the `feste_detail` endpoint's own 404 does) plus `/feste/nuova` (`requiresAuth: true`); `FesteDetailView.vue` edits in place exactly like `ReportDetailView.vue` (an `editing` ref swaps to `FesteForm.vue`), rather than a separate edit route.
- `definitions/interfaces/` mirrors the DRF serializers — update these together with serializer changes (e.g. `User` has no `email`; `Report`/`ReportPin`/`ReportStatus` in `Report.ts`, `Category` in `Category.ts`; `Poll`/`PollOption` in `Poll.ts` with `null` counts while results are hidden; `Feste`/`RoutePoint`/`CardImage` in `Feste.ts`, since C2). Since C3, `Content.ts` holds the shared `CardBase`/`MapPoint`/`CardImage`/`ContentItem` shapes the four generic type apps build on (`Chiesa.ts`/`Ricetta.ts`/`Personaggio.ts`/`Termine.ts`/`Libro.ts` each extend `CardBase`) — `Feste.ts` keeps its own literal copy rather than being retrofitted onto it. Since C4, `CardBase` (and `Feste.ts`'s own copy) also carries `language`/`translation_of`/`translations` (`TranslationRef`, defined in both files for the same reason). Status labels/colors and the admin transition map live in `lib/reportStatus.ts`; `lib/apiErrors.ts` flattens `{detail, fields}` bodies for forms.
- Polls UI is inline in `PollsView` (no detail route): `components/polls/PollCard.vue` renders every state (vote radios / result bars / login prompt / admin close), `PollForm.vue` is the admin create form. Beware `PanelBox` is `h-full` — a stacked column needs `self-start` on its grid item or every panel stretches to the row height (fixed on every stacked view in M4: `PollsView`, `HomeView`, `ReportDetailView`).
- Components emit instead of mutating props (`ReportForm` emits `saved`/`cancel`) — `vue/no-mutating-props` is lint-enforced.
- **Accessibility conventions (ADR-0005, since 2026-08-22):** every `<label>` pairs with its input via `for`/`id` — the old `<label>Text</label><br />` followed by a separate `<FormInput>`/`<textarea>`/`<select>` with no association is the pattern being retired, not one to copy into new forms. Purely decorative icons (nav icons next to visible text, the search-icon SVG) get `aria-hidden="true"`; icon-only or ambiguous-text buttons (route/image remove `✕`, the rich-text toolbar's single-letter buttons) get `aria-label`, and toggle buttons get `:aria-pressed`. Each view has exactly one `<h1>` — `MainTitle.vue` — never `SiteTitle.vue`'s header wordmark, which is chrome on every route, not page content. Status/semantic colors must clear 4.5:1 against their text (checked via the WCAG relative-luminance formula, not eyeballed) — see `lib/reportStatus.ts`'s comment for the shades this ruled out.

## Commands

### Running the whole app (repo root)

```sh
scripts/dev                   # backend :8000 + frontend :5173, bare metal, one Ctrl-C
docker compose up --build     # Postgres + backend :8000 + frontend :5173
```

`scripts/dev` is the fast path: SQLite, `DJANGO_DEBUG=true` exported for you
(TD-11), pending migrations applied first, and it refuses to start when either
port is taken rather than half-starting (`API_PORT=8001 WEB_PORT=5174` moves
them). It kills the whole process tree on exit — `runserver` forks the real
server as its own child and vite runs under npx, so killing only what the
script launched leaves the port held. Use compose when you want Postgres.

### Backend (venv at REPO ROOT: `.venv`)

```sh
python3 -m venv .venv && source .venv/bin/activate    # first time
python -m pip install -r backend/requirements-dev.txt # runtime + pytest/ruff

cd backend
export DJANGO_DEBUG=true                              # once per shell (see below)
python manage.py migrate
python manage.py runserver                            # http://127.0.0.1:8000

python -m pytest                                      # canonical test runner
python -m pytest account/tests.py -k collaborate           # subset
python -m ruff check .                                # lint (must be clean)
```

`python manage.py test` exits 0 but runs nothing (tests are pytest-style) — use pytest.
Shared fixtures (`api_client`, `citizen`, `other_citizen`, `admin`, `make_client`,
autouse `media_root` and a throttle-cache reset) live in `backend/conftest.py`;
`PASSWORD` is in `common/testing.py`. Under pytest, `settings.py` swaps in the MD5
password hasher (TD-05) — that is why the suite runs in ~3s rather than ~50s.

**`DJANGO_DEBUG` defaults to false (TD-11).** Forgetting the variable fails safe
instead of serving tracebacks, and outside debug/test the app raises
`ImproperlyConfigured` unless `DJANGO_SECRET_KEY` is set — it is SimpleJWT's
signing key, so a committed fallback meant anyone could mint an admin token.
Development opts in: `docker compose` passes `DJANGO_DEBUG=true`, bare-metal
shells `export` it, and pytest is exempt via the `TESTING` flag (which is also
why the HTTPS hardening block carries `and not TESTING` — without it
`SECURE_SSL_REDIRECT` answers the test client with a 301).

Create a superuser via `python manage.py shell`:

```python
from account.models import User
User.objects.create_superuser(email="admin@example.com", password="password123", name="admin")
```

### Frontend (run from `frontend/`)

```sh
npm install
npm run dev              # Vite dev server on :5173

npm run test:unit        # Vitest
npm run test:unit -- src/stores/__tests__/user.spec.ts   # single file
npm run test:e2e:dev     # Cypress against the Vite dev server
npm run test:e2e         # Cypress against a production build (build first)

npm run lint             # oxlint --fix, then eslint --fix --cache
npm run format           # prettier --write --experimental-cli src/
npm run build            # type-check (vue-tsc) + vite build
```

Three entries in `.claude/launch.json` for the in-app browser: **`backend`**
(Django on :8000 against the bare-metal SQLite database, `DJANGO_DEBUG=true`),
`frontend` (Vite dev server, :5173) and **`frontend-preview`** (:4173, `vite
preview` over `dist/`). Start `backend` too — the SPA is empty without its API.
It calls the venv's Python directly rather than `scripts/dev` because the
in-app launcher cannot execute that script (exit 126, "Operation not
permitted"), and it does not run migrations: if a branch brought new ones,
`python manage.py migrate` first. Verify anything Leaflet-related against the
**preview**, not the dev server — the default-import gotcha and TD-02's
bundling only show in built output, which is also why CI runs Cypress there.

`npm run format` is `prettier --write src/`. The tree **is** prettier-clean as
of 2026-08-27 (`npx prettier --check .` passes, `eslint.config.ts` included),
so running it now touches only what you changed — it used to rewrite ~30
unrelated files, which is why it was kept out of the definition of done until the tree was clean. **It is part of it since 2026-09-10** (TD-35): CI runs `npx prettier --check .` in the frontend job, check-only.
Keep it that way: if a formatting sweep ever becomes necessary again, it goes
in its own commit, never mixed into a feature diff.

### CI

`.github/workflows/ci.yml` runs the gates on every push to `main` and every pull
request: backend (ruff + pytest, Python 3.14, SQLite fallback) and frontend
(oxlint + eslint + prettier `--check` + vitest + build, Node 22). A third job runs
Cypress against a **production build** (`npm run test:e2e`, vite preview on
:4173) — not the dev server, because TD-02's double Leaflet bundle and the
default-import gotcha only appear in built output. It must stay green. Two
things to know before editing it: the linters run **check-only** there, because
`npm run lint` applies `--fix` and would let CI pass by rewriting files; and the
e2e job **starts a real Django API on :8000** (TD-04, 2026-08-30) seeded by
`python manage.py seed_e2e` — a DEBUG-only management command, since it creates
accounts whose password is written down in a spec file — and, since
2026-09-04, **clears what the v1 flows leave behind**: a vote is final by
design (F3.2) and an upvote is a toggle, so a suite re-run against a database
nobody re-seeded fails on the app behaving correctly. Re-seed between runs; the
first assertion to go red is the poll's "results are hidden before you vote".
The SPA's API base URL
defaults to `http://127.0.0.1:8000`, which is why the build needs no extra
environment there; it would if the API ever moved. **Running the e2e suite
locally needs the same API *and* `CORS_ALLOWED_ORIGINS` pointed at the preview
origin** — the default is `:5173`, so without it every request from `:4173` is
blocked by the browser while the server cheerfully logs a 200, and the specs
fail as if the data were missing:

```sh
cd backend && export DJANGO_DEBUG=true E2E_MAIL_DIR=/tmp/vivere-gaeta-mail \
  CORS_ALLOWED_ORIGINS='http://localhost:4173,http://127.0.0.1:4173'
python manage.py seed_e2e && python manage.py runserver 127.0.0.1:8000 --noreload
# then, from frontend/, after a build:
#   E2E_MAIL_DIR=/tmp/vivere-gaeta-mail npm run test:e2e
```

`export`, not a prefix, and it matters: `VAR=x a && b` gives the variables to
`a` only, so the command this block used to print started `runserver` with
neither DEBUG nor the preview origin — the very failure the paragraph above
warns about (found 2026-09-10). **`E2E_MAIL_DIR` must name the same directory
for the API and for Cypress**: the API writes its mail there as files (DEBUG
only, a branch production cannot reach) and the `inviteLink` task in
`cypress.config.ts` reads the invite back, so `collabora.cy.ts` can follow a
request through the author's approval in Django admin to a collaborator who
signs in. `seed_e2e` makes its admin a Django superuser for that reason, and
clears every `@e2e.test` request and `invito-…` account a previous run left.

**The WCAG 2.1 AA gate lives in that same job** (`cypress/e2e/a11y.cy.ts`,
2026-08-31): axe-core through `cypress-axe`, ADR-0005's four WCAG tags and no
`best-practice` rules, over seventeen cases — *Il progetto*, the welcome page, a tour, the tour form and the form drawing on the map joined on 2026-09-18 — (plus two cases that are about keyboard order rather than axe: the skip link, and the map's pins staying out of the tab order) including an authoring form
*showing a real validation error* and the same form *with images attached* (the
images strip does not exist until it has images, so nothing measured it before). Two things it will teach you the hard way if
you extend it: it excludes `.leaflet-container`, which since 2026-09-05 is ADR-0005's *decision* (the text lists are
the conforming path) rather than a deferral; and it waits for the wordmark's fade-in to finish before
measuring, because axe samples whatever colour the compositor is showing and
mid-animation that is a real reading of a meaningless value. A green run is a
floor, not a conformance claim: while the auth forms' labels were unassociated
the gate passed them anyway, because an input takes an accessible name from its
`placeholder` — ADR-0005 is what got them fixed, not a red build. **Watch the login
count when adding specs:** the endpoint is rate limited to 10/min per IP
(TD-18) and a whole run spends eight of those (the invite journey in `collabora.cy.ts` signs its new collaborator in once), so a new suite that signs in per test
turns the whole run intermittently red with a 429 the SPA reports as a wrong
password. The a11y spec's collaborator block uses `testIsolation: false` and
one `before` login for that reason. **`cy.session` is not the answer here**:
refresh rotation with a blacklist means a cached snapshot replays a token the
previous test already spent, and the app correctly logs itself out — tried,
and it fails as 401s on every write.

**The lint policy is pinned, in both languages, and that is deliberate** (TD-33, 2026-09-05). `backend/pyproject.toml` selects ruff's rules explicitly (`select = ["E4", "E7", "E9", "F"]` — 0.15's defaults, written down) because with `select` implicit a version bump redefines what "clean" means: 0.16 would have arrived with 81 findings on unchanged code. `frontend/.oxlintrc.json` still selects the `correctness` *category* — new correctness rules are closer to bug reports than to opinions, and pinning rule ids there would be unmaintainable — but its `overrides` now scope the **vitest** plugin to `src/**/__tests__/**`, because applied repo-wide it reads the Cypress specs and reports every Chai `expect(x).to.equal(y)` as an "unknown modifier". Widening either set is a separate PR from bumping either tool: a lint sweep has an opinion in it, and a bump should not.

Two upgrades are **held back on purpose**, with the reasons in TD-06 rather than in a lockfile nobody reads: **cypress 16** (`cypress-axe@1.7.0` peers only up to 15, so the a11y gate's plugin would go to an invalid peer state — and 16 counts a `.sr-only` element as visible, which `login.cy.ts` asserts against) and **typescript 7** (`vue-tsc` cannot load it: `ERR_PACKAGE_PATH_NOT_EXPORTED`).

Definition of done for a feature (spec §9): the spec section it implements is referenced, pytest + Vitest green, ruff + eslint + prettier (`--check`) clean, `npm run build` green. CI runs all of it on every push and pull request.

## External agent skills (agent-skills + impeccable)

Two third-party skill packs are used with this repo, installed as **Claude Code
plugins (never vendored)** so nothing of theirs is committed here:
[addyosmani/agent-skills](https://github.com/addyosmani/agent-skills) (engineering
process: spec → plan → build → test → review → ship) and
[pbakaus/impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable) (frontend design
quality; 59 deterministic detector rules, `.vue` supported). They do not conflict
with each other — one covers process, the other aesthetics — but they do carry
their own conventions, which this project overrides as follows.

**Install scopes** (2026-08-19). agent-skills is installed at **`local` scope,
pinned to this repo** — declared in `.claude/settings.local.json`, which
this repo's `.gitignore` excludes, so the marketplace reference stays out of the
public repo on every clone and the pack loads in no other project. (Until
2026-08-19 it was covered only by a machine-level `~/.config/git/ignore`, so the
guarantee did not travel — DOC-04.)

impeccable stays a **`user`-scope** install and is currently *disabled*; enable it per session when you actually want a design
pass. Manage both with `claude plugin ... --scope local` from the repo root;
`claude plugin list` shows what is active. Plugins load at process start, so a
scope change only takes effect in the next session.

**Precedence.** The project workflow wins. agent-skills injects a routing
meta-skill at every session start whose behaviours are phrased as
"non-negotiable"; where it disagrees with [docs/mvp-spec.md](docs/mvp-spec.md) §10
(spec first, one goal per session, session log, human ownership) or with the
commands above, this file is authoritative.

**Where artifacts go.** agent-skills defaults to writing `tasks/plan.md` and
`tasks/todo.md` at the repo root — **don't**. `docs/mvp-spec.md` is the scope
authority, planned work goes in [docs/roadmap.md](docs/roadmap.md), debt in
[docs/tech-debt.md](docs/tech-debt.md), and expensive-to-reverse decisions in
[docs/adr/](docs/adr/README.md). Per-task planning files are churn: this repo
has no session logs, by decision (2026-08-20).

**Definition of done** stays the project's own (spec §9): pytest + ruff + Vitest
+ eslint + prettier `--check` + `npm run build`. A skill's generic checklist does not replace it.

**Language.** Both packs write English by default; UI copy in this app is Italian
(N4). Any copy a skill proposes for the SPA must be translated before it lands.

**Scope guard — lifted 2026-08-22.** `/impeccable audit` and agent-skills'
`frontend-ui-engineering` both push for a full WCAG pass. That used to be
advisory here, because v1's N5 had scoped it out and the question was still
open. It is **decided now**: WCAG 2.1 AA, whole app
([ADR-0005](docs/adr/0005-wcag-2.1-aa-accessibility-bar.md)), so their contrast
and keyboard findings land against a bar this project has accepted — check them
against the ADR's Consequences, which says what is already fixed and what is
deliberately deferred, rather than dismissing them as out of scope.

**Adopted from the pack:** `documentation-and-adrs` — its README structure
(name, one-paragraph description, Quick Start, Commands, Architecture) and its
ADR format and rules on when one is worth writing. See `docs/adr/README.md`.

**Skills not to use here.** Enabling the pack loads all 24 skills, so the
exclusions are rules rather than missing files:

- `spec-driven-development` and `planning-and-task-breakdown` — **evaluated
  2026-08-20 and not adopted**, deliberately, as this file's own rule required.
  The reason is worth keeping: the six areas of the pack's spec template are
  Objective, Commands, Project Structure, Code Style, Testing Strategy and
  Boundaries — five of which are *this file*. It overlaps CLAUDE.md, not
  `docs/mvp-spec.md`, and it has no equivalent of a product spec (F1/F2/F3,
  non-goals). It also mandates `tasks/plan.md` + `tasks/todo.md` per task. So:
  `docs/mvp-spec.md` stays the product spec, CLAUDE.md stays the working
  agreement. Do not run both systems. (Same call the author made in the
  `next-cmp-settings` project, for the same reason.)
- `browser-testing-with-devtools` — requires the `chrome-devtools` MCP server,
  which this setup does not have; use the built-in browser preview instead.
- `performance-optimization` — premature here. Measure after the demo is live
  and only against a real complaint.

**Which pack skill is worth reaching for next** (as of 2026-08-27, with the v2

**impeccable specifics.** `.impeccable/` is gitignored (live-mode config + cache).

Detector baseline at setup time (2026-08-19), **one real finding, one false
positive** (re-checked in the 2026-08-19 deep audit):

- Real: `SiteTitle.vue` uses a purple gradient, flagged as the classic
  "AI-generated UI" tell — it is the M4 brand, so changing it is a deliberate
  product call, not a defect.
- **False positive:** the reported `text-gray-600` on `bg-green-100` in
  `PollCard.vue:91` does not exist. That line is a ternary and the pairs that
  actually render are `bg-gray-200 text-gray-600` and `bg-green-100
  text-green-700`; the detector combined classes across the `?:`. Do not spend a
  session on it, and treat ternary class bindings as a known blind spot of the
  contrast rule.
